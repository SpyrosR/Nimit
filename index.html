Background & Philosophy

About

I am a Bachelor of Management Studies (B.M.S.) student at Jain University, Bangalore, actively working as a passionate video editor and colorist powered by DaVinci Resolve. Balancing creative storytelling with commercial awareness, I craft visual experiences that hook viewers from the very first frame.

My work focuses heavily on music-led edits, clean rhythmic pacing, cinematic color grading, and fundamental videography & cinematography principles. Whether cutting together a live band performance or refining promotional material, I ensure every cut feels musically synchronized and visually cohesive.

To expand my creative toolset, I am currently diving into Blender for 3D elements while simultaneously preparing for the CMA-USA examinations. This unique combination instills ruthless work discipline, accurate analytical planning, and an unwavering commitment to meeting strict client deadlines.

How I Work

Process

A transparent 5-step pipeline from raw clips to final master delivery.

01

Brief

We align on your creative vision, references, target audience, music track, and project deadline.

02

Footage Review

Ingest media into DaVinci Resolve, verify codecs, organize selects, and flag the strongest takes.

03

Rough Cut

Assemble the story with tight beat synchronization, rhythm, narrative flow, and scene progression.

04

Feedback

Share a private review link to gather your notes and implement targeted adjustments quickly.

05

Color/Audio Polish & Final Delivery

Execute the cinematic color grade, clean up audio/levels in Fairlight, and deliver high-res masters.
